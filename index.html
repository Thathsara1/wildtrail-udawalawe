Our Story

Born beside the wild.

WildTrail was started by guides who grew up on the edge of Udawalawe National Park. We know its roads, its seasons, and its elephants the way most people know their own neighbourhood — because it is one.

Every safari we run is locally operated, small in scale, and built around genuine respect for the animals we share the park with. No large convoys, no rushing — just time in the wild, done properly.
